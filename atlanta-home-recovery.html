How It Works

1. Tell Us the Situation

Share the recovery timeline, mobility needs, home layout, and ZIP code. Do not send protected medical records through a general inquiry.

2. Review Practical Options

We help organize suitable equipment and services based on availability. Final medical equipment decisions should follow the treating clinician’s direction.

3. Arrange the Home

Confirm pricing, delivery, setup, and any separate partner services before work begins.
