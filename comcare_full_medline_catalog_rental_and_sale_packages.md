## Remove Or Do Not Add To Rental

These products are better as sale items, add-ons, or should be avoided unless you have a specific workflow:

- Disposable incontinence products
- Disposable wound care products
- Shoe horns, reachers, hip kits, and small ADL tools
- Slings, if you prefer each patient to own their own for hygiene
- Commodes, unless you have a strict sanitation/rent-to-own process
