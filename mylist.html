Medicare & Insurance Enrollment Checklist

1. Complete CMS 855S Application (Medicare DME Enrollment)

CMS‑855S is the official Medicare enrollment application for DMEPOS suppliers. Without this approval, most commercial insurers will not contract with you.
Your accreditation certificate (ACHC, HQAA, or Joint Commission)
Your $50,000 surety bond
Your business license
Your facility inspection readiness
Your policies & procedures
Your inventory and equipment cleaning logs
Your posted hours and signage
Your NPI
Your liability insurance
What you must submit:
CMS 855S form (online via PECOS or paper)
Copy of accreditation certificate
Copy of surety bond
Copy of liability insurance
Copy of business license
Floor plan of facility (optional but helpful)
Photos of facility (clean/dirty zones, office, signage)
Ownership documents (Articles of Organization, EIN letter)
Timeline: 45–90 days after submission, depending on contractor workload.
Why it matters: Medicare approval is the gateway to Medicaid and commercial insurance contracts.

2. Register with PECOS (Provider Enrollment, Chain, and Ownership System)

PECOS is Medicare’s online portal for submitting and managing your 855S application.
Create an account linked to your NPI
Start a new 855S application
Upload all required documents
Pay the Medicare application fee
Track your application status
Important: PECOS requires your NPI to be active before you can begin.

3. Obtain NPI Number (National Provider Identifier)

A unique 10‑digit ID number required for all healthcare suppliers.
Apply through NPPES (National Plan & Provider Enumeration System)
Legal business name
EIN
Business address
Taxonomy code for DME supplier (332B00000X)
Why it matters: Your NPI is used on Medicare 855S, Medicaid applications, commercial insurance contracts, claims and billing, provider directories.

4. Apply for Insurance Network Participation (Commercial Payers)

Once Medicare approves you, you can apply to private insurers.
Medicare approval letter
Accreditation certificate
Liability insurance
Surety bond
W‑9
NPI confirmation
CAQH profile (must be complete and attested)
Business license
Product list (what DME you provide)
Service area map
Delivery policy
Return/repair policy
Typical timeline: 60–120 days depending on the payer.
Important: Some insurers will not contract with new DME companies until you have 6–12 months of operational history. Medicare approval helps, but commercial payers still evaluate risk.
