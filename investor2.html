ComfortCare Home Medical
Investor Presentation

Business Overview

ComfortCare Home Medical provides durable medical equipment (DME) rentals, delivery, setup, and patient support in Metro Atlanta & Sandy Springs. The business operates from a modern flex-warehouse and leverages Curri Logistics for on-demand, trackable delivery.

Market Opportunity

  • Growing demand for home-based care and DME in Atlanta
  • Fragmented market with outdated pricing models
  • ComfortCare offers modern logistics and transparent pricing

Revenue Levers

  1. Raise Weekly Price: Industry average $150–$220/week (current: $45/week)
  2. Charge Setup, Delivery, Pickup Separately: Industry: $100–$200 each (current: $45 delivery, $150 setup, $0 pickup)
  3. Minimum Rental Period: 4-week minimum, $250–$350 base package

Growth Tools

  • Profitable pricing model and cost calculator
  • ComfortCare pricing sheet for transparency
  • Profit projection dashboard for investors
  • Accreditation & compliance for payer contracts

Operations & Accreditation

  • Accredited DME provider (ACHC / HQAA / Medicare)
  • Compliant with all state and federal regulations
  • Modern facility: office, warehouse, cleaning, and logistics hub
  • Curri Logistics for fast, reliable delivery

Financial Projections (Sample)

  • Revenue growth with new pricing model
  • Improved margins with minimum rental period
  • Scalable operations for expansion
