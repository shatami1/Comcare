Comfort Care Investor Overview

Business Model & Opportunity

Comfort Care provides mobility equipment rentals, delivery, setup, and customer support in Metro Atlanta and Norcross. Our focus is on wheelchairs, walkers, rollators, knee scooters, canes, and related mobility solutions.
Key Revenue Levers:
  • Raise weekly rental price to industry average ($150–$220/week vs. current $45/week)
  • Charge setup, delivery, and pickup separately (align with industry: $100–$200 each)
  • Implement a 4-week minimum rental period ($250–$350 base package)

Growth Tools & Investor Value

  • Profitable pricing model and cost calculator
  • Comfort Care pricing sheet for transparency
  • Profit projection dashboard for investors
  • Accreditation & compliance for payer contracts
