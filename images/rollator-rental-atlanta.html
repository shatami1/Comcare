Rollator FAQs

What is the difference between a walker and a rollator?

A standard walker is usually more stable but must be lifted or moved carefully. A rollator has wheels and often includes a seat and brakes.

Who should use a rollator?

Rollators may work for users with enough balance and control to safely manage wheels and brakes. Follow medical guidance for your situation.
