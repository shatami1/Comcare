ComfortCare DME Accreditation & Operations Master Plan

1. Company Overview

ComfortCare Home Medical provides durable medical equipment (DME) including hospital beds, lift chairs, wheelchairs, walkers, bathroom safety equipment, and respiratory support devices. The business operates from a WareSpace Atlanta flex‑warehouse unit, which serves as the administrative office, equipment storage, cleaning area, and logistics hub.

ComfortCare uses Curri as its on‑demand delivery fleet for fast, reliable, and trackable equipment delivery.
